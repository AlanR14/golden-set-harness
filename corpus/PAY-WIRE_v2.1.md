## 3.1 When a callback is required
A callback is required for any outgoing wire above $10,000 requested by email or phone. The callback must go to the phone number on file, never to a number supplied in the request.

## 3.2 Recording the callback
Record the callback time, the number dialled and the employee name in the wire log before releasing the wire.
