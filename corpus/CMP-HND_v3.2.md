## 2 Logging complaints
Every customer complaint is logged in the complaint system within 1 business day of receipt, whether it was received verbally or in writing.

## 3 Escalated complaints
A complaint that alleges discrimination is escalated to Compliance the same business day. Do not attempt to resolve it at the branch.
