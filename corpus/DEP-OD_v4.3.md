## 5.3 Waiver eligibility
A branch manager may waive an overdraft fee once per rolling 12 months per account. Branch manager approval is required for every waiver. Each waiver must be logged in the case system with the reason code.

## 5.4 Delinquent accounts
Overdraft fee waivers are not available for accounts more than 60 days delinquent. Refer these accounts to Collections.
