## 2 Reporting gifts
Any gift or entertainment from a vendor or customer valued above $100 must be reported to the Ethics Office within 5 business days.

## 3 Cash gifts
Cash or cash-equivalent gifts, including gift cards, are never accepted, whatever the amount.
